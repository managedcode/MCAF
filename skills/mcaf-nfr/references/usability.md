# Usability

Define whether real users can complete the affected task accurately, efficiently, and with recoverable errors. Use [accessibility](accessibility.md) alongside usability where relevant.

## Evidence

- Representative actors, goals, starting states, devices, and required assistance.
- Task completion, time/steps, errors, abandonment, and observed confusion.
- Clear labels, feedback, loading/empty/error states, and recovery.
- Consistency with supplied designs and established product flows.

Use rendered interaction checks and representative user evaluation when requested or needed for an unresolved usability question. Source/DOM inspection alone does not prove visible layout or usability. Record observations and specific changes rather than broad taste claims.
