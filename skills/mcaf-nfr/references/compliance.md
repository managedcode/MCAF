# Compliance Requirements

Use the organization's confirmed obligations for the actual data, jurisdiction, and operating model. Do not infer certification or mandate a framework from a generic checklist.

## Record

- Applicable obligation and authoritative source supplied or confirmed by its owner.
- Affected data, purpose, retention, access, deletion, residency, and third parties.
- Required controls, operational owner, audit evidence, and review/approval.
- Testable product behavior and limits of technical verification.

Map each obligation to feature requirements, implementation, tests, and evidence where applicable. Keep sensitive information out of logs and test artifacts. Verify current legal/standard details with authoritative sources when the task requires interpreting them; involve the responsible owner for unresolved obligations.

Passing tests or a baseline security review does not establish legal compliance or certification.
